Family controls in Medka

See how child sign-in, team invitations, and clip consent are described by Medka. Use the live app to review the controls available to your account.

Access and privacy

Family code and PIN
The child signs in on their device using the code and PIN set up by a parent. The account setup and sign-in for adults are separate.
Team access
Parents invite teachers or clinicians and manage access to their child’s information from the Team screen.
Camera use and consent
Medka says it uses the camera on the device during play to notice gaze and attention. The Team screen shows an opt-in to keep six-second clips as evidence for a clinician.
What the child sees
The child sees games and stars. Medka keeps progress scores, reports, and labels in the adult views.
Family review and deletion
Medka says families can review notes and summaries and request deletion of their data in the app.
Advertising and data sales
Medka’s public site says it carries no advertising and does not sell family data.

What the child sign-in looks like

The child’s device asks for a family code and a PIN. Parents set up the PIN in Settings in the live app.

For questions about camera permissions or stored information, contact Medka’s support team.
