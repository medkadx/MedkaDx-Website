Account setup and child access

Choose your role when you start. Parents, teachers, and clinicians enter a name and mobile number and ask Medka to send a sign-in code. Existing adult users go to Log in.

On the child’s device, open the child sign-in and enter the family code and PIN from the parent’s account.

Common questions

Does Medka replace a therapist?

Medka supports practice at home and gathers observations. The clinician sets goals, reviews progress, and decides which changes to approve.

Can my child use Rafeeq if they don’t speak yet?

Yes. The live app describes picture choices that children can tap. Rafeeq encourages responses by tapping, making a sound, or gesturing as the child works toward communication goals.

Which devices does Medka support?

Medka describes access through a browser on a phone, tablet, or laptop. Its site says camera and voice features work best on a phone or tablet.

What happens to video during play?

Medka describes live observation on the device and a parent consent control for keeping short clips. See the Safety and privacy guide for the controls shown in the app.

What ages do the two products cover?

The live Medka app describes guided play for ages two to ten. Medka DX’s proposed screening workflow is for children aged 18 to 72 months with developmental concerns.

Which languages are available?

The adult interface offers Arabic and English. Medka describes Rafeeq speaking Gulf Arabic in the child’s view.

How can a clinic learn about Medka DX screening?

Email KaRama iT about the proposed caregiver intake and clinician screening workflow. Visit the care team guide for the live Medka clinician and teacher tools.
