One roadmap for the child’s care team

Medka brings games, family check-ins, classroom observations, and clinician goals into a shared view of what a child has reached and what they are practising.

Roadmap screenFive areasMilestone evidence

The roadmap at a glance

The parent view shows activity counts such as stars, sessions, minutes, and streak. Below those counters, milestones are grouped by area and marked as reached, now, or next. The view also shows recent changes and space for notes from teachers and clinicians.

Scores shown in the example are demo values. The roadmap is a record of practice and observations for the care team.

Five areas of development

Character
The child’s interests, strengths, triggers, and what helps them settle give adults and Rafeeq context.
Skills
Current goals can include asking with a word or symbol, naming objects, or expanding spoken vocabulary.
Attitude
Goals cover regulation and participation, such as settling with support or waiting for a turn.
Social
Goals can include attention, turn-taking, greetings, and recognising emotions.
Learning
Examples include identifying colours and practising numbers or attention skills.

What sits behind a milestone

Select a milestone in the live app to see its current state and the activities or observations behind it. Practice results, caregiver entries, and professional notes can contribute evidence.

Medka describes an engine that looks for changes or stalled progress and proposes a next step. Clinicians review proposed changes and decide which to approve.
