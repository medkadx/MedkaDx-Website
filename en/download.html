Get the Medka app

Medka is available as a web app. You can use it directly in your browser or add a shortcut to your phone’s home screen.

Use Medka in your browser

Add Medka to your home screen

iPhone and iPad

Open Medka in Safari. Use Share and select Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if you see that option, then tap Add.

Apple’s guide

Android

Open Medka in Chrome. In the menu, choose the option to add a shortcut to your home screen, then tap Add.
