MEDKA DX

Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, or Edge. Saudi Arabic is the default.
Use the language switch to open the English version of the same page.
Keep the HTML pages, en folder, styles.css, site.js, and assets together.

The website content, navigation, and screenshot viewer work locally.
The Download now button opens the official browser app guide and explains
how to add Medka to your phone’s home screen.
